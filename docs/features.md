# Features

[Documentation](README.md) · [Getting started](getting-started.md) · [Roadmap](roadmap.md)

Helix's direction is a Pokémon journey built around learning how the world
works. Care, practical experience and a companion's history should matter to
the breeder you become. These are early development systems, with a broader
breeding journey still ahead.

## Companions with continuity

The local game begins in a laboratory and introduces persistent first
companions. Individual records make a Pokémon's identity and history part of
the journey. This is an initial foundation, not a complete lifecycle system
for every species.

## Knowledge earned in the field

Field investigations connect the laboratory to places and people outside it.
Village work gives companionship a practical role: shared watering is one
implemented activity whose progress the game remembers. The broader direction
is for habitats, NPC motives and useful work to support discovery.

## Predict, measure, revisit

A candidate laboratory lesson introduces MIRA and ADA, the Culture Reader and
Nanomon Medium. Make a prediction, compare how an indicator culture responds
to its conditions, then return to the saved result.

[public terminal demo](getting-started.md).

## Toward extraordinary lineages

The long-term goal is breeder mastery: enduring companions, meaningful care
and lineages shaped by what you learn. Pokémon rarity, professional tools
and complementary specializations are design goals. General breeding and
broad native lifecycle coverage remain unfinished.

## What is available today?

The public repository runs the synthetic assay and local host service, and
provides a patch/overlay for the Emerald source. It does not reproduce the
full local game shown here; required generated game packages are absent.

The opening, companion records and village work belong to the accepted local
baseline. The laboratory lesson is a verified local candidate. Exact version
status, storage limits and handheld work are tracked in the
[roadmap](roadmap.md) and [verification record](verification.md).
