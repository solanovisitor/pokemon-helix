# Community conduct

Welcome curious players, artists, scientists and developers of all experience
levels. Be patient, give specific constructive feedback, credit others' work,
and respect different backgrounds, identities and abilities.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and posting
private data without consent are not welcome. Critique an idea or a change,
not the worth of its author. Respect requests to stop an interaction.

This applies to repository issues, pull requests and other project spaces.
Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict
participation according to the severity and repetition of the behavior.
They must follow the same standards and consider reports fairly.

Use GitHub's **Report content** / **Report abuse** controls for sensitive conduct
reports. For non-sensitive moderation needs, ask a maintainer in the relevant
thread without repeating harmful or private material. Do not use public issues
to publish personal details or evidence that could expose another person.
